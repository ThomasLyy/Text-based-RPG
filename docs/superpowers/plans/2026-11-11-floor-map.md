# Floor Map Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Each act becomes a branching map of 10 rooms (Fight, Elite, Event, Shop, Rest) plus a boss, and the player picks the route.

**Architecture:** A new pure module `src/game/map.ts` generates an act's map from `Math.random`. `gameLoop` in `src/game/game.ts` walks it, asking `io.ask` which reachable room to enter, then runs that room (fight, elite, event, merchant or rest). The UI draws the map from a `MapView` that is added to `CombatView`, so tests and balance bots drive everything through `io.ask` as today.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vite, Vitest, vanilla DOM (no new dependencies).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-floor-map-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No new dependencies.
- All in-game text in English. Room labels are words, not icons: `Fight`, `Elite`, `Event`, `Shop`, `Rest`, `Boss`.
- 3 acts. Each act has 10 rooms, then the boss: 33 floors in all. Act names: `The Sewers`, `The Catacombs`, `Hell`.
- 4 lanes (0-3). Each floor has 2 to 4 rooms. A room links to 1 to 3 rooms on the next floor, in its own lane or a neighbouring one. Links never cross. Every room can be reached from floor 1 and has a way out.
- Room odds on every floor: Fight 45%, Elite 15%, Event 20%, Merchant 10%, Rest 10%. Override: at least one room on floor 9 or 10 is Rest.
- No fixed rules for the first floors.
- No health back after fights: the 20% rest and the campfire before the boss are removed. Full heal (health and mana) only when entering the next act after a boss.
- Elite: 1 or 2 of the act's normal enemies, ×1.4 toughness (health and damage), named `Elite <name>`, double XP (so double gold). Reward quality one step up (Common→Uncommon→Rare→Epic, capped). You can flee an Elite.
- Enemy count: floors 1-3 give 1-2 enemies, floors 4-7 give 2, floors 8-10 give 2-3. Toughness ×(1 + 0.03 × (floor − 1)).
- Rest room: choose one of `Rest` (40% of max health, rounded, plus all mana) or `Forge` (today's forge).
- Event room: one random event out of shrine, trial, Strange brew, Locked chest.
- Target: about 20-minute runs, about 3 turns per fight for the sensible bots.
- Branch: `develop`. One commit per task.

## Review Focus

- **Forge with nothing to upgrade** (all Epic or empty): the Rest room must grey out `Forge`, not let the player waste the room. Test in Task 3.
- **Strange brew −2 Strength at full health:** max HP drops, so current health must be clamped to the new max. Test in Task 3.
- **Constant random numbers** (tests mock `Math.random` to 0.5): map generation must still finish and keep every rule. No retry loops. Test in Task 1.
- **Fleeing an Elite:** no reward, and the path continues from that room. Test in Task 4.
- **Dying in an event:** events must never kill (trap and offering floors at 1 HP). Covered by the existing trial and shrine tests plus the chest test in Task 3.

`map: every rule holds over 200 seeded maps`. For seeds 1..200 with the `seeded` helper:
  - `map.length === 10`

Expected: FAIL, because `./map` doesn't exist.

- [ ] **Step 3: Implement `generateMap()`**

This algorithm guarantees the rules without retries:

```
walks: A confined to lanes 0-1, B confined to lanes 2-3 (they never meet, so every floor has ≥2 rooms),
       plus 2 free walks over 0-3. Each walk starts at a random lane in its range.
each step: the walk moves to lane-1, lane or lane+1 (inside its range), chosen at random among the moves
           that do not cross an already-chosen link (a→a+1 crosses a+1→a; straight moves never cross).
rooms = union of visited (floor, lane); next = union of the walks' moves out of that room.
kinds: roll per room with the odds; then, if floors 9-10 have no rest, turn one random room on floor 9 or 10 into rest.
```
